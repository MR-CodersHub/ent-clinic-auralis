This privacy notice describes how Auralis ENT Clinic (“Auralis”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information. It applies to our website, appointment forms, newsletters, patient portal and clinical services.

1. Who we are

Auralis ENT Clinic is an independent specialist ear, nose and throat clinic based at 18 Harrogate Road, Chapel Allerton, Leeds LS7 3PD. We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

Our Data Protection Officer can be contacted at privacy@auralisclinic.com or by post at the clinic address marked “Data Protection Officer”.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in three broad ways:

  • Information you give us — name, contact details, date of birth, appointment preferences, insurance details and any message you send.
  • Clinical information — symptoms, medical history, examination findings, test results, treatment plans and correspondence relevant to your care.
  • Technical information — limited website data such as browser type, pages visited and approximate location, collected through privacy-respecting analytics where consent is given.

We do not intentionally collect information from children through this website. Children’s clinical records are created with parental or guardian involvement.

3. How we use your information

  • To arrange, provide and follow up clinical care.
  • To communicate with you about appointments, results and treatment options.
  • To process payments, insurance claims and billing queries.
  • To maintain accurate clinical records and meet our legal obligations.
  • To send the Auralis Note newsletter or other updates where you have opted in.
  • To improve the clinic’s services through anonymised service evaluation.
We will never use your clinical information to target advertising, and we do not sell personal data to any third party.

4. Our legal bases

Depending on the activity, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Provision of health care — to deliver safe clinical services.
  • Contract — to fulfil the appointment or care plan you book.
  • Legal obligation — for example, record-keeping and safeguarding requirements.
  • Legitimate interests — to run and improve the clinic securely, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — for marketing emails and optional analytics. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only where necessary and with appropriate safeguards. This may include:

  • Clinicians and staff directly involved in your care.
  • Laboratories, imaging providers and other specialists you are referred to.
  • Your GP or insurer, where you have agreed or where the law requires it.
  • Trusted service providers such as our secure records platform, payment processor and email provider.
  • Regulators or authorities when legally compelled.

All service providers act under written contracts and may only process information on our instructions.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website uses only the cookies necessary for basic functionality, such as remembering your theme and writing-direction preferences. These are stored locally in your browser and are not used for advertising.

If we enable optional analytics in future, we will ask for consent first and provide a clear way to change your choice. We do not use cross-site advertising trackers.

7. How we protect your information

  • Encrypted clinical records with role-based access controls.
  • Multi-factor authentication for staff accounts.
  • Secure, encrypted connections for online forms and the patient portal.
  • Regular access reviews, staff training and incident-response testing.
  • Physical security at the clinic, including restricted records areas.

If a breach occurs that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the relevant regulator as required.

8. How long we keep information

Clinical records are retained in line with professional and legal requirements. For adults, this is generally eight years after the last appointment; for children, until their 25th birthday. Financial records are kept for six years. Marketing preferences are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

When information is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal information.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete information where there is no legal reason to keep it.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing or optional analytics.
  • Request portability of information you provided to us.

To exercise any right, email privacy@auralisclinic.com. We respond within one month and may ask for identification to protect your records.

10. Complaints and contact

If you have a concern about how we have handled your information, please contact our Data Protection Officer first so we can investigate. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

For general clinic questions, contact hello@auralisclinic.com or call +44 113 496 0720.
