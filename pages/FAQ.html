Appointments

No referral is required for most private Auralis appointments. If your insurer requires one, our reception team can explain exactly what they need before you book.
Most first appointments are available within 5–10 working days. Concerns that our clinical team considers urgent are triaged and may be offered sooner.

Billing & insurance

Fees start at $95 for a hearing assessment and $110–$130 for most ENT consultations. Service pages list exact tiers, and our pricing page explains care plans.
We work with major insurers. Please check your policy, obtain pre-authorisation where required, and bring your membership details to the appointment.
Care plans can be paid monthly. Selected hearing aid packages also have interest-free instalment options. Ask our billing team for a written quote before committing.
