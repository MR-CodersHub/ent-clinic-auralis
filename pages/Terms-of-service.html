3. Appointments

  • An online form is a request, not a confirmed booking. Our team will confirm by phone or email.
  • Please provide accurate contact details and arrive at least five minutes early.
  • If you are unable to attend, tell us as soon as possible so the slot can be offered to another patient.

4. Fees and payment

Consultation fees are quoted on our pricing and service pages or in writing before your appointment. Payment is due at the time of the appointment unless a plan or insurer arrangement has been agreed in advance.

  • Insurance patients are responsible for any shortfall, excess or non-covered amount.

5. Cancellations and changes

Appointments cancelled with more than 48 hours’ notice are fully refunded or moved at no cost. Cancellations with less than 48 hours’ notice may incur a charge of 50% of the appointment fee. Missed appointments may be charged in full.

If Auralis cancels an appointment, you will receive a full refund or a priority replacement slot.
